# Unsubbed Listen

**A free alternative to Speechify.** Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no subscription, no tracking. Your files never leave your device.

**Use it now:** https://unsubbed.github.io/listen

## What it does
- Reads your text aloud with the voices already on your device, and marks the sentence and word being spoken
- Click any sentence to start reading from there
- Speed from 0.5× to 3.0×, with words per minute shown
- Keyboard: Space plays and pauses, ← / → skip one sentence, [ / ] change the speed
- Opens .txt, .md, .html, .docx and .epub files right on your device, with no upload
- A Shelf of your documents that remembers where you stopped in each one
- Works offline once loaded, in light or dark mode
- Open and save your work as ordinary files on your own device

## Known limits
- No MP3 download. Browsers can't record their own speech voices.
- No PDF yet.
- The voices depend on your device and browser. Some devices need a speech voice installed.
- If you turn on online voices, your browser sends the text to its maker (Google, Microsoft or Apple) to speak it. On-device voices are the default.

## Privacy
Everything happens on your device. The app makes no outside network requests: no analytics, no ads, no server. Your work is stored in this browser until you clear it, and you can save a copy at any time.

## Run it yourself
It's a plain web page. Download this repository and open `index.html`, or serve the folder with any static web server.
